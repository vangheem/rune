- **Wayland: a decorated window's content rounds its bottom corners under the client-side frame.** The adwaita frame's headerbar rounds the top two and its shadow has always cut a 10-point rounded window, but the content's surface was square, so the bottom two corners showed past the frame's one-point border. The frame's border now follows the same arcs (`vendor/sctk-adwaita`, square while maximized or tiled like the headerbar) and says what radius the content leaves it (`winit`'s `WindowExtWayland::content_corner_radius`), so a decorated Wayland window asks for a translucent surface and clears outside those two arcs (`window::corners`, one draw of two corner squares a frame). Windows, macOS, borderless windows, X11, a square frame, and a surface with no alpha mode paint exactly what they did.
